# OpenFactory Program

The OpenFactory Program defines how people, companies, builds and hosted services become
OpenFactory-certified, and how the project is funded by those certifications. The software is
Apache-2.0 and may be used, modified and sold by anyone. The OpenFactory name and marks are
reserved for what passes the program's requirements ([NOTICE](../../NOTICE)).

**Status:** version 1.0, proposed 2026-09-21 for adoption by the maintainers. Fees and headcounts
are first-year proposals. [07-sustainability.md](07-sustainability.md) describes who may change
them. The platform itself is pre-1.0; every document here defers to [STATUS.md](../STATUS.md) on
current functionality.

## How the pieces fit

1. A customer engages a certified partner, or follows the method alone on the Light profile.
2. The partner's Solution Architect runs qualification and assessment and writes the design
   record. The Implementer installs, onboards each project, proves and rehearses the deployment,
   runs a costed pilot and hands over a runbook to the Operator.
3. The customer's developers (Associates) write cards and review pull requests. A Product Owner
   may switch on the product role.
4. The partner's support desk supports the deployment to a tier of the standard and keeps it
   current.
5. Each person holds the certification for the role they perform. The partner holds a tier
   based on certified staff and referenceable deployments. A hosted service holds a conformance
   certification against the hosting standard.
6. The project earns from certifications, partner fees, conformance listings and courseware
   licences, not from customers. The marks and the program move to a neutral holder when the
   ecosystem reaches the trigger defined in 07.
