## The participant agrees that

1. the marks "Certified OpenFactory", "OpenFactory Certified Hosting" and "OpenFactory
   Compatible" are owned by the project's steward (and its successor holder) and are licensed,
   not transferred, for use only with the qualifying offerings above while a certification is
   current;
2. results are submitted within ninety days of an offering's first public use and are
   reproducible by an end user following the submission's `README.md`;
3. marketing states the platform version the certification was granted against;
4. the offering does not remove or weaken any property in the conformance table, and any
   modification is described as such;
5. the marks are removed within thirty days of a certification lapsing or being revoked, and
   combination names cease to be used;
6. for a hosting offering, the hosting standard is met and the annual hosting audit is
   accepted;
7. for an add-on, critical fixes are shipped within two business days and others within ten,
   and the listing may be withdrawn if not;
8. the listing fee, where one applies, is paid annually in advance;
9. the steward, and its successor holder, may enforce the marks, and this form is the whole of
   the participant's licence to them.
