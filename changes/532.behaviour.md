**`openfactory doctor` sees a container filling up with processes.** The new check `pid_headroom` reads what the container it runs in and the panel hold against their `pids.max`. It fails at half of it, at a hundred zombies, or when a running container is asked and does not answer, and it gives the repair. Each line names the container as its service declares it, with the new `OPENFACTORY_ROLE` that the compose file sets to `worker` and `panel`.
