**An Azure Boards project can be limited to one Epic.** A tracker takes `scope_epic`, the id of an Epic, and the project's pickup queue, its board as the panel and the product role read it, its list of cards and the search for an already filed card of the same title are limited to the work items under that Epic, at any depth, so a team that keeps several Epics no longer has a card another person approves picked up by the project of a different one. The scope only narrows what the team's area paths select. An Epic that is not a number, names nothing the credential can read, has more than 1,500 work items under it or cannot be read reads as an unreadable board and the warning names the option, never as a project without a scope. A scoped project refuses, by name, to edit, close, remove, queue or rank a card outside its Epic. Unset, the whole team is read as before. From the cockpit, a project scoped by `scope_epic` or `scope_jql` opens the panel's own board, which shows the same slice; every other project opens its provider's board.
