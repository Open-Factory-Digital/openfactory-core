**The reads of the API are served under `/api/v1/`, and their old addresses are deprecated.** Each read that declares its shape — the floor, the jobs, a job's detail, the board, attention, the inbox, the budget, the decisions, the loops, the messages, the product room, its documents, the metrics, whoami and the action catalogue — answers at `/api/v1/…`, and its address without `v1` answers the same body as an alias marked deprecated in the API's description. The aliases answer through the 0.7 series and are removed no earlier than 0.8.0, so a screen of one's own should call `/api/v1/` now; the default panel keeps working unchanged. The description is committed as `docs/reference/openapi.json`, with the package's version, and `docs/reference/the-front-end-api.md` says the deprecation policy.
