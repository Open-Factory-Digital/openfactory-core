**`openfactory certify`: a deployment's evidence pack.** `openfactory certify deployment` writes the pack a deployment submits to the implementation partner program: its controls computed from the deployment and its diagnostics, redacted so the practitioner is the only name it carries, and re-read before it is written; nothing is written without `--yes`. `openfactory doctor --json` and `openfactory box status --json` are the versioned documents it carries. The pack is not signed yet, and says so.
