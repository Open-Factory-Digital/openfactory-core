**The worker and the panel reap the processes they inherit.** Their PID 1 was our Python process, which reaps no orphan, so every child git's detached maintenance left behind stayed a zombie for the container's life: a panel ran out of processes after 39 hours of use and read the engine as unreachable until it was restarted. Every container whose PID 1 is ours now starts an init: `init: true` in `docker-compose.yml`, `docker run --init` for the box, and `tini` as the worker and sandbox images' entrypoint, for a runtime that adds none.
