**Forgetting a project closes the engine runs that still held what was said.** After `project forget` or `project forget-conversations`, the product's conversation runs in the durable engine stayed open, holding the forgotten messages and replies where the engine's retention never reaches them. Both commands now close the product's conversation runs that no turn is at work in, and `project forget` the project's tech-lead run too; the report says when their history expires, and the next message starts a new run.
