**Under an Azure Boards `scope_epic`, the cards the factory files must be put under the Epic by other means.** A split's children, the product role's cards and an impediment are created in the project and linked to no parent, so they are outside the scope: the poller does not pick them up, the panel and the product role do not show them, and the search for an earlier filing does not find them, so a retry files another, and a scoped project refuses to edit, close, remove, queue or rank them until they are. File the cards you want worked under a Feature of the Epic yourself. Starting a job on a card by its number, reopening a closed card and commenting on one still reach any work item of the project.
