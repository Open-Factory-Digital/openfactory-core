**`openfactory certify verify` checks a pack offline, and the pack measures its outcomes.** `verify` checks a pack the way the submissions bot does, its files, schema, checksums, signature and every threshold, each reported by id. The pack's outcomes over its window, from jobs and how they ended to cost per merged ticket, are read from the journals and the metrics store: a measure that cannot be read is null with its reason, never zero.
