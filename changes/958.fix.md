**On a fresh install, everything that opens the brand-new board file at once gets in.** The worker, the panel and a CLI verb opening `board.db` before it existed raced for its one-time switch to WAL, and the loser could be refused with `database is locked` after 0.4 s; that switch now waits for a neighbour's lock up to the board's usual 5 s busy timeout, asks again only when the refusal is a lock, and logs a warning naming the bound when it does give up.
