**Who may act, and who approves production, is proposed to become one decision the core makes (ADR-0057).** Nothing changes in this release. The record proposes that a production release is approved by the signed-in person holding an `approver` role, with a fresh sign-in, and that `~/.openfactory/approvers.json`, `OPENFACTORY_APPROVERS` and `openfactory approver` retire over three releases. It also lists how today's panel, board and product tokens, OIDC groups, `project.admins`, `product.admins` and `prod_approvers` would map onto roles on upgrade, so that nobody loses access silently. Read it before you add approvers to the password store.
